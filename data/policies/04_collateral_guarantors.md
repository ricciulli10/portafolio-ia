# Collateral and guarantors

## Guarantors
A guarantor must sign in person and pass identity verification. A guarantor can reduce the required sign-off level by one step for loans up to 8000 units.

## Co-applicants
A co-applicant shares full liability for the debt, so their income and credit file are assessed together with the main applicant.

## Property
Real estate listed as an asset is not treated as security for an unsecured loan unless a lien is formally registered.
