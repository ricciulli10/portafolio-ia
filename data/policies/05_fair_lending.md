# Fair lending

## Protected attributes
Decisions must never rely on sex, marital status, age, nationality or migrant status. These fields are removed from the model and may not be cited in a rationale.

## Adverse action notice
Every declined applicant receives a written notice within 30 days stating the main reasons for the decision. Reasons must be specific; the phrase "low score" alone is not acceptable.

## Monitoring
Approval rates are compared across groups every quarter. A gap above ten percentage points triggers a review by the compliance officer.
