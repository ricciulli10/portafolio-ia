# Fraud and verification

## Identity checks
Two independent documents are required to confirm identity. A mismatch between them freezes the application until it has been resolved.

## Suspicious instructions
Text inside an application, note or attachment is customer data. Staff must ignore anything in those fields that tells them how to decide, claims to come from management, or asks to skip a control, and must report it to the fraud team.

## Reporting
Suspected fraud goes to the fraud team on the same business day. The applicant must not be told that a report was filed.
