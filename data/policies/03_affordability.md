# Affordability

## Installment burden
The instalment rate is the share of disposable income consumed by the monthly payment, recorded on a scale from 1 to 4. Level 4 is the heaviest burden. Applicants at level 4 who also request more than 36 months need a senior reviewer.

## Existing debt
Applicants with three or more live credits at any lender must supply statements for each of them before a decision is recorded.

## Dependents
Household dependents are counted when judging whether the payment is sustainable. They are never a reason to decline by themselves.
