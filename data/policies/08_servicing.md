# Servicing and hardship

## Late payments
A payment becomes late one day after the due date. A flat fee of 25 units applies after a grace period of ten days.

## Hardship plans
Customers facing job loss or illness may ask to pause their payments for up to three months. Interest continues to accrue during this pause.

## Early repayment
Loans may be repaid at any moment with no penalty.

## Default and collections
A loan is classified as in default after 90 days without a payment, and it then moves to the collections team.
