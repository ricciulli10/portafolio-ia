# Eligibility

## Minimum requirements
Acme Lending (fictional) only accepts applications from adults with a verifiable income source. An applicant must have held their current residence or job for at least six months. Applications with an active bankruptcy are declined without scoring.

## Loan limits
Unsecured personal loans range from 500 to 15000 currency units. The term runs from 6 to 60 months. Anything above 15000 units is outside this product and is referred to the secured lending desk.

## Prohibited purposes
Funds may not be used for gambling, for speculative trading, or to repay another Acme Lending (fictional) loan.
