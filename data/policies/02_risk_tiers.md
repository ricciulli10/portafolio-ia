# Risk tiers

## Model score usage
The credit model produces a calibrated probability of default. An analyst must not change that number by hand. The score informs the decision but never replaces the written policy.

## Automatic approval band
A loan may be approved by an analyst alone only when the probability of default is below the published cutoff and the amount does not exceed 5000 units.

## Review band
When the probability of default is at or above the cutoff, the case cannot be approved on the analyst's own authority. It moves to a senior reviewer, who may approve it with a documented reason or decline it.

## Large exposures
Every loan over 5000 units needs a second signature from a senior reviewer, even when the score is low.
